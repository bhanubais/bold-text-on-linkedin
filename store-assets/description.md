## Detailed Description

You're drafting a LinkedIn post. You want a key takeaway to stand out. Naturally, you highlight the line and press **Ctrl+B** (or **Cmd+B** on a Mac).

Nothing happens.

LinkedIn's web editor still doesn't support the standard keyboard shortcut for bold text.

To format text today, most people leave LinkedIn, open a third-party font generator tab, paste their draft, copy weird Unicode symbols, and paste them back.

That wastes time. Worse, those fake Unicode fonts break screen readers for visually impaired colleagues and scramble LinkedIn's search index.

**LinkedIn Rich Text Shortcuts fixes this with one keystroke.**

Highlight your text, hit **Ctrl+B** (or **Cmd+B** on Mac), and your words turn bold immediately. Hit the shortcut again to remove bold. That's all there is to it.

---

### How to Use It in 3 Steps

1. Highlight any text inside a LinkedIn post or comment.
2. Press **Ctrl+B** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+B** (macOS).
3. Your text turns bold instantly. Hit it again to unbold.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this work on Mac?**  
Yes. Press **Cmd+B** just like you do in Google Docs, Word, or Slack.

**Does this work in comments and messages?**  
Yes. It works in LinkedIn post creators, comment boxes, replies, and message threads.

**Why doesn't this extension support italics or underline?**  
LinkedIn's editor only supports bold formatting natively. Tools that offer italics or underline use fake Unicode character hacks. Those hacks break screen readers and hurt search ranking. We stick strictly to what LinkedIn's editor natively supports so your posts stay clean.

**Does this extension collect or view my data?**  
No. The extension runs 100% locally in your browser. It doesn't send data anywhere, doesn't use cookies, and doesn't connect to external servers.

**Do I need an account or subscription?**  
No. Install the extension and start formatting right away. No sign-ups, no fees.
