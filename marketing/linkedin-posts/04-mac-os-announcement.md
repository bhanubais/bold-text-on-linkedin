## Post Copy

Dear Mac users: this one is specifically for you.

How many times have you highlighted text in a LinkedIn post and instinctively tapped **Cmd+B**?

Only to watch your Chrome bookmarks bar flash open instead?

Or worse: nothing happens at all.

You use Cmd+B everywhere else on your Mac:
- Apple Notes
- Notion
- Google Docs
- Slack
- Bear and Craft

Your muscle memory expects Cmd+B to just work.

Yet the moment you open LinkedIn, that instinct breaks. You are forced to stop typing, grab your mouse, or head to some spammy font generator website.

When we updated **LinkedIn Rich Text Shortcuts** to version 2.0, fixing Mac parity was our top priority.

Full Mac support is now live:
- Press **Cmd+B** on any highlighted text in LinkedIn.
- Your text turns bold instantly.
- Press **Cmd+B** again to remove the bold style.
- No bookmark bar collisions.
- Works across post drafts, comments, replies, and direct messages.

Best of all, it triggers LinkedIn's built-in rich text engine. It does not paste fake math Unicode characters that scramble screen readers or ruin search indexability.

Under the hood:
- Written in pure JavaScript (zero heavy frameworks).
- Fully Manifest V3 compliant.
- Less than 50 KB in total size.
- Zero analytics, zero tracking, zero data storage.

If you write posts on a MacBook or Mac desktop, your keyboard muscle memory is finally restored.

Grab it for free on the Chrome Web Store. The direct link is in the first comment.

What other Mac shortcut do you miss most when using web apps?
