### Slide 2: The Hidden Flaw (The Unicode Trap)
- **Asset File:** `design/social-banners/carousel/carousel-slide-2.png`
- **Headline:** External Font Generators Don't Make Real Letters
- **Core Explanation:**
  - When you copy bold text from a random font website, you aren't copying formatted text.
  - You are copying Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols from the Unicode table (like U+1D400).
- **The 3 Hidden Consequences:**
  1. **Accessibility Disaster:** Screen readers literally read "Mathematical Bold Capital H" instead of "Hello".
  2. **Search Index Penalty:** People searching for your keywords will never find your post.
  3. **Visual Glitches:** Devices missing those glyph fonts display empty rectangular boxes (tofu symbols).

### Slide 3: The Secret (The Hidden Native Engine)
- **Asset File:** `design/social-banners/carousel/carousel-slide-3.png`
- **Headline:** LinkedIn Already Supports Real Bold
- **Core Explanation:**
  - LinkedIn's web editor uses HTML contenteditable under the hood.
  - The native rich text engine renders clean, accessible `<strong>` tags without breaking fonts.
- **The Catch:**
  - LinkedIn built the engine, but forgot to hook up the standard keyboard shortcuts (`Ctrl+B` and `Cmd+B`).
  - So millions of creators thought third-party generator websites were their only option.

### Slide 4: The Solution (1 Keystroke Fix)
- **Asset File:** `design/social-banners/carousel/carousel-slide-4.png`
- **Headline:** Meet LinkedIn Rich Text Shortcuts
- **How It Works:**
  - Step 1: Highlight any text in your post or comment draft.
  - Step 2: Press **Ctrl+B** on Windows/Linux or **Cmd+B** on Mac.
  - Step 3: Done. Your words turn bold instantly.
- **Key Advantages:**
  - Works on PC and Mac.
  - Works in feed posts, comments, replies, and DMs.
  - Retains 100% accessibility compliance.
