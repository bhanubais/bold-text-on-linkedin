## 2. Maker's First Comment (Launch Day Kickoff)

*(Word count: ~280 words. Formatted for high engagement and founder authenticity.)*

Hey Product Hunt community! 👋

I'm Bhanu, the creator of **LinkedIn Rich Text Shortcuts**.

If you write posts on LinkedIn, you have probably experienced this exact frustration:

You finish writing a sharp sentence. You want one key phrase to stand out. Instinctively, you highlight the text and press **Ctrl+B** (or **Cmd+B** on a Mac).

And absolutely nothing happens.

Google Docs has supported Ctrl+B for 18 years. Word has supported it for 30 years. Slack, Notion, and every modern text area support it natively.

Yet on LinkedIn, millions of people were forced to use an absurd workaround:
1. Open a new browser tab.
2. Search for a sketchy "font generator" website.
3. Paste text to copy pseudo-Unicode symbols.
4. Paste those symbols back into LinkedIn.

When I looked into LinkedIn's editor code, I realized those generator websites do not even make real bold letters. They paste Mathematical Alphanumeric Unicode glyphs. That causes two serious problems: it breaks screen readers for visually impaired colleagues, and it renders your post unsearchable in LinkedIn's search index.

Worse yet, LinkedIn already had a native rich text engine built in! They just never hooked up the keyboard shortcut.

So I built a simple, lightweight fix.

**LinkedIn Rich Text Shortcuts** gives you native bold formatting with one keystroke:
- Highlight any text in a post, comment, reply, or DM.
- Press **Ctrl+B** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+B** (macOS).
- Instant native bold text. Hit it again to unbold.

No tracking. No analytics. No heavy frameworks. Under 50 KB of pure vanilla JavaScript.

It is 100% free and open-source on GitHub.

I would love to hear your feedback. What editor shortcut do you wish other web apps supported?
