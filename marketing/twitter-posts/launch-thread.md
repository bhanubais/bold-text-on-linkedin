### Tweet 3 (The Discovery)

Here is the wild part.

LinkedIn's web editor already has native rich text built into its contenteditable engine.

The LinkedIn team simply never hooked up the standard keyboard shortcuts.

Millions of creators have been copy-pasting broken math glyphs for zero reason.

---

### Tweet 4 (The Solution)

Meet LinkedIn Rich Text Shortcuts:

1. Highlight any text in your post or comment draft
2. Press Ctrl+B (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+B (macOS)
3. Instant, clean bold text

Hit the shortcut again to remove bold. One second. Zero friction.
