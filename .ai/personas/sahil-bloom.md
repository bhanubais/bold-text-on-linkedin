- Complex ideas deserve simple explanations. Break everything into digestible steps.
- Visual frameworks and mental models outperform walls of text.
- Cross-post strategically — LinkedIn, Twitter/X, newsletter. Same insight, adapted format.

### Voice
- Warm, educational, approachable.
- Uses numbered lists, step-by-step breakdowns, and clear takeaways.
- Ends with a call-to-action that feels like an invitation, not a demand.
