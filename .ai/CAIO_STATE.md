## 2. Venture Invariants & Architectural Rules

1. **Zero Direct Execution:** The CAIO never writes code, copy, or graphics directly. CAIO scaffolds topology, maintains macro-state, updates `.ai/` infrastructure, and provisions composite sub-agents with clinical spawn prompts.
2. **Topological Boundary:**
   - Production extension code strictly lives in `/src/`.
   - Build archives live in `/dist/`.
   - Store copy and compliance documentation strictly in `/store-assets/`.
   - Visual designs, banners, and screenshots strictly in `/design/`.
   - Marketing campaigns, posts, and launch content strictly in `/marketing/`.
   - The Swarm Brain lives in `/.ai/`.
3. **Execution Runtime:** Standardize strictly on **Bun** (`bun test`). Node is deprecated.
4. **Punctuation Standard:** Absolute zero tolerance for em-dashes (`—`) or en-dashes (`–`). Use clean punctuation: commas, periods, colons, parentheses, or simple hyphens (`-`).
5. **Product Boundary:** Only bold (`Ctrl+B` on Windows/Linux, `Cmd+B` on macOS). Italic and underline are strictly prohibited.
6. **Agent Tracking Protocol:** Every sub-agent must:
   - Check off tasks in its `.ai/agents/<agent-name>.md` file.
   - Record an execution log entry with timestamps.
   - Update status to 🟢 Completed in `AGENTS.md`.
