# Skill: Product Hunt Launch Day Playbook

## Purpose
Orchestrate a successful Product Hunt launch from prep to post-launch.

## Pre-Launch (T-14 to T-1)
- [ ] Create Product Hunt maker account
- [ ] Build "Coming Soon" page
- [ ] Prepare gallery: 5 images (first = hero), optional GIF
- [ ] Write tagline (≤60 chars)
- [ ] Draft maker's first comment (200-400 words)
- [ ] Recruit 10-15 early supporters to upvote + comment on launch day
- [ ] Schedule LinkedIn and Twitter teaser posts
- [ ] Prepare email to personal network

## Launch Day (T-0)
- [ ] Submit at 12:01 AM Pacific Time
- [ ] Post maker comment immediately after going live
- [ ] Share on LinkedIn (personal + company page)
- [ ] Share on Twitter/X with visuals
- [ ] Respond to EVERY comment on PH within 30 minutes
- [ ] Post an update at T+6 hours ("We're #X on Product Hunt today!")
- [ ] Post end-of-day thank you

## Post-Launch (T+1 to T+7)
- [ ] Write a "What we learned" LinkedIn post
- [ ] Respond to all remaining PH comments
- [ ] Update extension listing with PH badge if top 5
- [ ] Analyze traffic sources in Chrome Developer Dashboard
