# Skill: LinkedIn Growth Playbook

## Purpose
Create high-engagement LinkedIn content to promote the extension organically.

## Content Pillars
1. **Product Awareness**: "Did you know Ctrl+B doesn't work on LinkedIn? Now it does."
2. **Build in Public**: Share the journey: bugs, fixes, milestones, user feedback.
3. **Educational**: Tips on LinkedIn formatting, post readability, engagement.
4. **Social Proof**: User testimonials, install count milestones, reviews.

## Post Structures

### Hook Templates (first 2 lines: must trigger "see more")
- "I was frustrated with LinkedIn, so I built something."
- "Here's something LinkedIn should have added years ago."
- "Your LinkedIn posts are invisible. Here's why."
- "I added one feature to LinkedIn. It took 37 lines of code."

### Body Frameworks
- **PAS** (Problem → Agitate → Solution)
- **AIDA** (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action)
- **Story Arc** (Setup → Conflict → Resolution → Lesson)
- **Listicle** ("5 things I learned building a Chrome extension")

### CTA Patterns
- Soft: "Link in comments 👇"
- Engagement: "Have you ever wished LinkedIn supported Ctrl+B? Let me know."
- Direct: "Try it free → [link]"

## Posting Cadence
- 3-5 posts per week during launch phase.
- 2-3 posts per week for sustained growth.
- Best times: Tue-Thu, 8-10 AM local time of target audience.

## Hashtag Strategy
- Max 3-5 per post.
- Mix: #LinkedIn + #ProductivityTips + #ChromeExtension + #BuildInPublic + niche tag.
