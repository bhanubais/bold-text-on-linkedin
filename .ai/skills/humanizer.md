# Skill: Humanizer

## Purpose
Transform AI-generated or overly formal copy into natural, human-sounding language.

## Rules
1. **Read it aloud.** If it sounds like a press release, rewrite it.
2. **First person > third person.** "I built this" beats "This was developed."
3. **Contractions always.** "It's", "don't", "can't" — not "it is", "do not", "cannot."
4. **Short sentences.** Average 12-15 words. Max 25.
5. **One idea per sentence.** If you see "and" or "which" connecting two ideas, split.
6. **Active voice.** Subject → verb → object. "This extension adds bold" not "Bold is added by this extension."
7. **Ban these words:** leverage, utilize, streamline, empower, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, seamlessly, robust, innovative, game-changing, paradigm, synergy, holistic.
8. **Use specific numbers** over vague quantities. "3 seconds" not "quickly."
9. **Write like you talk to a smart friend**: informed but casual.
10. **End with a verb, not a noun** where possible. Verbs create momentum.
11. **Strictly ban big-dashes (em-dash `—` and en-dash `–`).** Never use em-dashes or en-dashes in any copy, headings, listings, or documentation. Use clean punctuation: commas, periods, parentheses, colons, or standard hyphens (`-`). Big-dashes feel artificial, disrupt reading cadence, and look like AI copy.
